# What is Sqope?

**Sqope** is a feed of quantum computing news, research, and conversations, part of the [Qucan](../index.md) platform. Where [QCAP](../qcap/index.md) is structured learning and [Qompile](../qompile/index.md)/[Qanvas](../qanvas/index.md) are for building, Sqope is for reading and publishing — deep-dive articles and research from the quantum computing community, plus its own editorial coverage.

Follow creators, bookmark articles, publish your own findings, and keep up with the field without leaving the platform.
